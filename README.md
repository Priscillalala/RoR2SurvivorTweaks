# Survivors to groove with\~move with\~win with\~lose with\~loop with\~rush with\~loot with\~charge the teleporter with\~fall in love with
Survivors, tweaked by yours truly. Currently just Mercenary.. maybe just Mercenary forever..

## Context
I've mostly been playing [2R4R's](https://thunderstore.io/c/riskofrain2/p/RiskOfBrainrot/2R4R/) Extinction difficulty lately. Extinction really emphasizes crowd control so some of my balancing choices might reflect that.

## Changes
The following information is also **available in-game**, on the character select screen! Skill changes are listed in the `Skills` tab when hovering over each skill, and other changes are listed in the `Overview` tab.

## <img src="https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/images/Mercenary.png" width="32"> Mercenary

> Merc is a certified Hopoo Games banger who already plays really well in vanilla. I dislike how easy it is for Merc to spam skills into crowds - the Expose tweak is intended to address that. Focused Assault has also recieved a light rework to be a little weaker into crowds.

- Expose Cooldown Reduction: 1s ⇒ 0.5s

### <img src="https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/images/Laser_Sword.png" width="24"> Laser Sword

- Reduced 'floating' effect when striking enemies

### <img src="https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/images/Whirlwind.png" width="24"> Whirlwind

- Damage: 200% ⇒ 230%
- Air Hop Velocity: 9m/s ⇒ 10m/s

### <img src="https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/images/Blinding_Assault.png" width="24"> Blinding Assault

- Cooldown: 8s ⇒ 7.5s

### <img src="https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/images/Focused_Assault.png" width="24"> Focused Assault

- Cooldown: 8s ⇒ 7.5s
- Damage: 700% ⇒ 900%
- Damage is now split between all victims

### <img src="https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/images/Eviscerate.png" width="24"> Eviscerate

- Cooldown: 6s ⇒ 6.5s
- Evisceration Duration: 1s ⇒ 1.15s (hits 1 more time at base attack speed)
- Lingering Invincibility Duration: 0.6s ⇒ 0.4s
- Cooldown begins after fully eviscerating
- No longer targets allies
- Now prioritizes Exposed targets

### <img src="https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/images/Slicing_Winds.png" width="24"> Slicing Winds

- Cooldown: 6s ⇒ 6.5s
- Blades Attack Count: 8 ⇒ 7
- Blades attack rate now scales with attack speed
- Cooldown begins after casting
- Animation canceling into your secondary or utility starts the cooldown immediately
- Minor vfx and sfx improvements
